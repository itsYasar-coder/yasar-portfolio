Case study · Power BI + SQL

Sales dashboard: 2 days of Excel → 2 hours

Oct 2026 · 5 min read · Excel, SQL, Power BI

Client problem

A local shop tracked sales in 4 Excel files — different column names, duplicate bills, dates in 3 formats. Every month-end meant 2 days of copy-paste, and totals still didn't match.

What I did

  1. Clean (Excel): removed 400+ duplicates, TRIM + PROPER for names, fixed dates to YYYY-MM-DD, XLOOKUP to map product codes.
  2. Model (SQL): 3 views — monthly sales, top products, region summary.
  3. Visualize (Power BI): one page — 4 KPIs on top, trend line, top-5 bar, slicers for month / region / category, drill-through to bill detail.
-- monthly sales view
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', bill_date) AS month,
  region, SUM(amount) AS sales, COUNT(*) AS bills
FROM sales_clean
GROUP BY 1, 2;

Result

Reporting went from 2 days to ~2 hours. Owner now checks slow products weekly instead of monthly. Two decisions came directly from the dashboard: stop stocking 2 dead SKUs, push one combo offer.

Honest note: data was only 8k rows, so everything runs on free tools. No warehouse needed at this scale.
