Case study

How I saved 12 hrs/month with one dashboard

Sales Performance Dashboard — from messy Excel to auto-refresh Power BI. Full breakdown inside.

Problem

Shop owner tracked sales in 4 different Excel files. Monthly report took 2 days, numbers never matched.

What I did

  1. Cleaned 8k rows in Excel — dedup, fixed dates, XLOOKUP for product mapping
  2. Built 3 SQL views: monthly sales, top products, region summary
  3. Power BI: 1-page dashboard with KPIs, slicers, drill-through to invoice detail

Result
