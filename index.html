Case study

How one dashboard saved 12 hours a month

A walkthrough of the Sales Performance Dashboard — the problem, what I actually did, and what changed.

01 The problem

A local shop tracked sales across four Excel files with different column names, duplicate bills and dates written in three different formats. Every month-end meant two days of copy-paste — and the totals still didn't match the bank.

02 What I did

  1. Cleaned 8,000+ rows in Excel — removed 400 duplicates, standardised names, converted all dates to one format, and used XLOOKUP to map product codes.
  2. Modelled it in SQL with three views: monthly sales, top products and a regional summary.
  3. Built a single Power BI page — four KPIs at the top, a trend line, top-5 products, and slicers for month, region and category.

03 The result

Reporting dropped from two days to roughly two hours. The owner now checks weak products weekly instead of waiting for month-end, and two decisions came straight out of the dashboard: stop stocking two dead SKUs, and push a combo offer that was quietly outperforming.
